# O017 requirements and non-overlap

## Controlling role

D120 requires C20, C40, and B80. Its reader must teach paper and monograph
reading, source reconstruction, citation, expository writing, seminar notes,
errata, computational reproducibility, peer criticism, and bounded community
contributions.

## Hard boundary with B80

B80 teaches how to use Python, SageMath, SymPy, NumPy/SciPy, plots, tests,
notebooks, data, environments, and exact versus floating computation. O017 may
require those skills and inspect their research use, but it does not reteach
syntax, package APIs, numerical methods, plotting, or environment setup.

O017 instead teaches how to:

1. identify the status and dependency of a mathematical claim;
2. reconstruct an omitted proof step without silently strengthening a source;
3. locate, compare, cite, and preserve source authority;
4. write exposition whose claims can be audited;
5. distinguish proof, computation, example, counterexample, and heuristic;
6. formulate a reproducible erratum;
7. package computational witnesses with enough provenance to rerun them;
8. respond to substantive review without hiding unresolved uncertainty; and
9. make one bounded, lawful contribution to a mathematical community.

## Declared course order

1. Membaca argumen sebagai jejaring klaim.
2. Menelusuri literatur dan menilai otoritas sumber.
3. Merekonstruksi pembuktian yang diringkas atau dihilangkan.
4. Sitasi, provenance, dan buku catatan sumber.
5. Menulis eksposisi matematika yang dapat diaudit.
6. Komputasi yang dapat direproduksi sebagai bukti empiris.
7. Errata, koreksi, dan batas kepastian.
8. Catatan seminar, laporan penelaah, dan tanggapan terhadap kritik.
9. Kontribusi komunitas terbatas dan portofolio akhir.
